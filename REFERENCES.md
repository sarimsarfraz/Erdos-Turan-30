# Primary references

The repository is self-contained for its proofs and exact checks. These
references record the surrounding state of the problem as of 24 July 2026.

1. Jianfeng Hou and Hongbin Zhao, *Vector-valued smoothing for finite Sidon
   sets*, arXiv:2607.01169. The public manuscript proves an exact rational
   coefficient improvement within symmetric vector smoothing.
2. David B. Hulak, Arthur F. Ramos, and Ruy J. G. B. de Queiroz,
   *Formalizing the Prime-Field Singer Construction and Sidon Set
   Infrastructure in Lean 4*, arXiv:2605.03274. This records Erdős Problem 30
   as open and formalizes the conditional dependence on both an upper
   subpolynomial error and prime-gap input.
3. Daniel Carter, Zach Hunter, and Kevin O'Bryant, *On the Diameter of Finite
   Sidon Sets*, arXiv:2310.20032; Acta Mathematica Hungarica 175 (2025).
4. Sean Eberhard and Freddie Manners, *The apparent structure of dense Sidon
   sets*, arXiv:2107.05744.
5. James Singer, *A theorem in finite projective geometry and some applications
   to number theory*, Transactions of the AMS 43 (1938), 377--385.
